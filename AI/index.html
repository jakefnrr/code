How it works

Nodes are the steps of a conversation. Drag one from the palette and click the canvas to place it.
Connections — grab the green dot on a node's right edge and drag to another node. For Question nodes each connection becomes an option or a keyword route.
Question nodes work two ways: fixed multiple-choice options, or free text matched against keyword rules. Anything the rules miss goes to the AI brain, which answers from your knowledge base using free cloud AI (Pollinations) or on-device NLU.
Action nodes are automation: fire webhook POSTs or send emails when the flow reaches them.
AI writer — pick any message or question, click AI in the inspector, type what the step should do, and it writes the copy for you.
Automation triggers (Settings) listen globally — type "human" and any question hands off to your agent node.
Everything autosaves in your browser. Use Test to run the flow, and Export to share it as JSON.
