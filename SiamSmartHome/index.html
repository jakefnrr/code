SMART TECHNOLOGY

How Smart Homes Work

Smart homes combine connected devices, sensors and automation into one system.

Automation

Devices can automatically perform actions based on time, routines, sensors or other conditions.

Sensors

Motion, temperature, door, window and other sensors can help a home react automatically.

Mobile Control

Compatible devices can be controlled from a smartphone or tablet.

Voice Control

Compatible systems can allow people to control devices using voice commands.

Smart Scenes

One command can activate several devices at once.

Energy Management

Automation can help reduce unnecessary energy use.

Security Alerts

Connected sensors and cameras can provide notifications when something happens.

Future Ready

Smart-home systems can be expanded as new technology becomes available.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about smart-home technology.

What is a smart home?

A smart home uses connected devices, sensors and automation to control different parts of a home.

Can I control my home from my phone?

Many compatible smart-home systems allow devices to be monitored and controlled using a smartphone.

Can smart devices work together?

Depending on the devices and platform, different products can be connected into a larger smart-home system.

Can a smart home help save energy?

Automation can reduce unnecessary energy use by switching devices off or adjusting settings when they're not needed.

Does a smart home have to be complicated?

No. The best smart-home systems are designed around simple routines that make everyday tasks easier.
