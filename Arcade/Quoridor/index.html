How to play

Objective

Race across a board that is 9 tiles wide and 12 tiles tall. Player 1 (cyan) starts on the bottom row and wins by reaching any tile in the top row. Player 2 (magenta) starts on the top row and wins by reaching any tile in the bottom row.

Turns

  • Each turn you take exactly one action: move one tile, or place one wall.
  • There is no passing and no second action.

Movement

  • Step one tile up, down, left or right — never diagonally on a normal move.
  • Legal destinations glow cyan; jump landings glow dashed violet.
  • You are blocked by the board edge, by walls, and by the other pawn.

Cyan may take any glowing tile.

Jumping

  • Step into the opponent to hop straight over them onto the free tile behind.
  • If a wall or the board edge blocks that landing tile, you land one tile diagonally behind them instead — either side.
  • A wall never lets you jump into a space it seals, and you can never land on an occupied tile.

Cyan at the middle hops over magenta to the glowing tile.

Walls

  • Each player starts with 10 walls. A wall is two tiles long and sits in the seams between tiles, never on a tile.
  • A horizontal wall seals two vertical crossings at once; a vertical wall seals two horizontal crossings.
  • Drag a wall from your rack toward the board. It snaps to the nearest seam and previews green when legal, red when not.
  • Prefer tapping: tap a wall to pick it up, tap a seam, tap again to commit. R rotates, Esc returns it.
  • Rejected: overlaps, crossings at the same seam, off-board seams, and anything that leaves a player with no route to their goal.
  • A wall only leaves your rack once it is legally placed.

One cyan wall across two seams.

Winning

The game ends the instant a pawn steps onto its goal row — no further actions are allowed.
