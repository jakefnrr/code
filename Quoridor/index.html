HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: be the first to reach the opposite edge of the board. Cyan starts at the bottom, Magenta at the top.
  • Turns: each turn you either move your pawn one square (including diagonally when jumping an opponent) or place a wall.
  • Moving: legal squares glow cyan. You may not jump a pawn unless there's space to land beside or behind it.
  • Walls: each player has 10 walls. Drag a wall from the Wall Shop onto the board, or tap it and then tap the board. Tap ROTATE (or press R) to switch horizontal/vertical.
  • Wall rules: walls can't overlap or cross another wall, can't block the board edge, and can never seal off a player's entire route to their goal.
  • Winning: reach the far edge — the game ends instantly.
